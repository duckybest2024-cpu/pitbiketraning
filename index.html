Safety First, Always

Parents relax. We've thought of everything.

Riding should be a thrill, never a risk. Our entire program is built around keeping your child protected while they learn.

🪖

Full Safety Gear

DOT-approved helmets, goggles, chest protectors, gloves and boots — fitted and sanitised before every session.

🐢

Speed Limiters

Every beginner bike has an adjustable throttle limiter so kids can't go faster than their skill level.

🚧

Fenced, Off-Road Track

No public roads, no traffic. Just a soft-surface track designed and maintained for young learners.

🩹

Certified First-Aiders

A trained first-aider is on site for every session, with a clear emergency plan and equipment ready.

Our Safety Pledge

  1. No child rides without correctly fitted gear.
  2. Small groups — never more than 3 kids per coach.
  3. Every session opens with a safety briefing.
  4. We progress skills, not speed.
  5. Parents can watch from the viewing deck the whole time.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Does my child need any experience?

Not at all. The majority of our riders have never sat on a bike before. Our Tiny Treads and Taster sessions are designed completely around first-timers.

What should my child bring?

Just closed-toe shoes, long trousers, a water bottle and weather-appropriate layers. We provide the bike, helmet, and all protective gear.

Is it safe?

Safety is the foundation of everything we do: speed-limited bikes, a fenced off-road track, small coach-to-rider ratios, mandatory gear and on-site first aid. See our Safety section for the full picture.

How old does my child need to be?

We welcome riders from age 5 up to 15. Programs are grouped by age and ability so every child learns at the right pace.

Can parents watch?

Absolutely — we have a dedicated parent viewing deck overlooking the track. You're welcome to stay for the whole session.

What if it rains?

Light rain is usually fine and part of the fun. If conditions are unsafe we'll reschedule your session at no charge.
